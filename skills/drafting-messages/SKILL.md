# Drafting Messages

Makes a draft ready to copy and paste, and written for its reader.

## Rules

1. **Write the draft as plain text.** Never put it in a `>` blockquote, because the bars get copied with the text.
2. **Use full sentences.** The reader is not the user, so do not carry over a terse reply style. Keep the draft short.
